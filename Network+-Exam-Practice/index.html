Network+ (N10-009) practice exam simulator

A free, open-source simulator with original questions written from the public exam objectives. It runs entirely in your browser: no account, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere.

Exam mode

  • Up to 90 questions in 90 minutes. Performance-based questions come first, then multiple choice drawn by domain weight (23 / 20 / 19 / 14 / 24 %).
  • The clock never pauses. At 00:00:00 the exam ends by itself.
  • Flag questions, skip them, and come back from the Review screen at any time.
  • No explanations until you end the exam. Ending is final.
  • Afterwards: estimated score, pass or fail against 720, results per domain, objectives to revisit and a full answer review.

How it works

  • Navigation: Previous and Next move between questions. Review opens a grid of every question with its state (answered, unanswered, flagged); select a number to jump to it. Next on the last question opens the Review screen.
  • Performance-based questions: put steps in order, match items, or fill a subnetting table. Drag and drop with a mouse, or select an item and then select where it goes (works with touch and keyboard). Each one has a Reset button and can be skipped and revisited. These formats are our own design; CompTIA does not publish the formats used on the real exam.
  • Tools: turn on Highlight or Strikethrough, then select an answer option to mark it. A- and A+ change the text size.
  • Keyboard: Alt+N next, Alt+P previous, Alt+F flag, Alt+R review, 1 to 6 select an option.
  • Scoring is an approximation. Multiple choice is worth 1 point, all or nothing. A performance-based question is worth 3 points with partial credit (less than half right earns nothing). The share of points earned is mapped onto 100-900 with an assumed passing point of 75 % = 720. CompTIA does not publish its real method, so treat the result as a readiness signal, not a prediction.
  • Progress (attempt history and accuracy per objective) is kept only in this browser and can be erased from the Progress page.

The official exam objectives are published by CompTIA on its Network+ certification page. This site lists only objective numbers with short titles in our own words.

Honest practice

Every question here was written from scratch from the public objectives. None comes from a real exam, a book, a course or another practice site, and none should be presented as a real exam question. Please do not mix this tool with leaked exam content: it is against the certification rules and it teaches nothing.

In Exam mode, work alone, without notes or a search engine, so the estimated score means something. The answer key ships with this static site, so the only person who can be fooled is you.
